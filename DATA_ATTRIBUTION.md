# SILSO observation data attribution / 数据来源与许可

Data source: **World Data Center SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels**.

- Dataset: SILSO Sunspot Number Version 2.0 / monthly mean total sunspot number.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.24414/qnza-ac80
- Official monthly file: https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/DATA/SN_m_tot_V2.0.txt
- Dataset license: **CC BY-NC 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)
- Official citation guidance: https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/citations
- Included snapshot: `SILSO_monthly_1990_2025_snapshot.csv`, Jan 1990 to Dec 2025, access date 2026-10-24.

The inclusion of the original time-series snapshot does not relicense those data. Users should preserve the source credit and attribution and review the non-commercial restriction before commercial redistribution/publication. The derived charts and Python scripts are distinct from the SILSO data license.
